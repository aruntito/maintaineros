# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MaintainerOS aims to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming open-source community. Participation should be free from harassment and discrimination.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Provide actionable, constructive feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Accept that maintainers may decline work that is out of scope.
- Protect private information and avoid sharing secrets or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexualized comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or abusive conduct.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, and other spaces where a person is representing or participating in the MaintainerOS community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. The maintainer may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or take other action proportionate to the circumstances.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source community standards and the Contributor Covenant. It is a project-specific policy and is not represented as an unmodified official copy of another code of conduct.
